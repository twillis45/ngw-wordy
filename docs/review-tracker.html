Remaining work, in fix order

Effort is focused engineering days, not calendar days.

Finish accessibility → 8–9

Hints are pointer-event-only, so keyboard and AT users cannot use the hint system at all — and the row's aria-label describes an operation they cannot perform. Then: restore pinch zoom (userScalable:false is a documented WCAG 1.4.4 failure), announce rank changes, split the live region so a toast cannot clobber a result, fix the 1.01:1 clue-mode marker, drop max-[379px]:h-9 which shrinks targets on the smallest phones.

In progress ~2 d blocks: WCAG 2.2 AA

Onboarding → 9

The first screen explains scoring and never says what the game is. Teach the verb, keep the instruction until the first word lands, put a price on a hint before charging it, promote the Themes door out of a 13px muted line, fix the literal &rsquo; that renders in the assist copy, and stop telling phone players they can "just type it".

Next~2 d

Layout & hierarchy → 10

The only wing that can honestly reach a 10, because it is scored on measurements. secondary and muted are 0.3% apart in luminance — two of three contrast tiers are one tier. Section titles are the smallest, dimmest text in their own cards. The accent is spent eight times. Type ramp collapses to six named steps in rem, which also carries the dynamic-type half of the accessibility work.

Queued~3 d

Security, privacy & IP → 9–10

Publish privacy, terms and support routes — both stores hard-block without them, and with no analytics the policy is ten honest sentences. Drop dictionaryapi.dev: it has no terms, licence or privacy policy and currently receives every word a player taps. Swap Webster 1913 for WordNet, which resolves the licence question and the archaic-clue problem at once. Then CSP, the response.ok guard, LICENSE.

Queued~3 d

Game design & loop → 8–9

Clearing every row — what the UI tells you to do — earns rank 3 of 8, because ranks measure every word the letters can make. Re-anchor them. Ship escalating mode on by default; it is the idea that makes this structurally interesting and it is switched off. Raise the 45s assist and stop playing the win sound for a word the player didn't find. Generate 730+ puzzles so day 241 stops serving a solved board.

Queued~4 d

Interaction & feel → 9

Reduced motion still silences the prize and rank celebrations entirely — the fix landed only on bonus. Seven feedback events collapse to four distinguishable iOS patterns; spending a hint feels identical to banking a word. Muting audio also kills haptics, which is backwards. No master limiter, so the biggest moment is the one most likely to clip.

Queued~3 d haptics unverifiable without hardware

Deferred

A real device for haptics

The iOS haptic trick may only fire its first tick — the one inside the user gesture. If so the whole vocabulary collapses to one buzz and iOS haptics need Core Haptics, i.e. a native shell. Not testable headless.

Decisions on record

  • Platform: web to 9–10 first. Stores deferred, not abandoned — the same work clears Apple's 4.2 bar later.
  • Money: free now; a single one-time $4.99 unlock once themed content is deep. The only SKU a backend-less app can honestly support — consumables don't restore.
  • Themes: deferred pending the eight questions.
  • Never ship: a streak-freeze or streak-repair SKU. The streak resets hard to 1, which is honest while nothing is sold and predatory the moment something is.
