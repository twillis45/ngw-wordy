# Cultural Board — Black culture & African American experience

Seated 2026-08-10 for Wordy. Expands the standing single "Cultural Authenticity"
floating seat into a full bench of **28 seats across 8 sub-wings**, because one
seat cannot carry five culturally-specific packs and a 240-puzzle authoring
plan. Companion to `docs/REVIEW_BOARD_GAME_WING.md`. Keep in sync with the
`feedback_review_board_roster` memory.

## The rules this bench runs under

Unchanged from standing doctrine, and non-negotiable:

- **Lens-not-literal.** Each seat channels a named practitioner's publicly known
  body of work as a *perspective*. No fabricated quotes, and no claim that any
  individual reviewed anything.
- **Insider-accurate, never stereotype.**
- **Internal-diversity aware.** Black American culture is not monolithic —
  region, generation, class, denomination and institution all vary. A seat that
  flattens is failing.
- **ASK-DON'T-ASSUME.** Where a question is about the *author's* life rather
  than about culture, the bench frames the options; it does not invent an answer.
- **Anti-appropriation, anti-caricature, dignity-first.**
- **The bench can BLOCK content.**

## An honest limit, stated once

This is a structured-perspective exercise, not community consultation. It is
good for catching flattening, absence and cliché, and for generating candidate
material at volume. It is **not** a substitute for review by actual community
members before culturally-specific content ships commercially — especially at
the 96–240 puzzle scale this project is aiming for. Budget for at least one real
reader per pack.

## 1. FOODWAYS & THE TABLE (8 seats)
Covers The Cookout, Sunday Dinner, Fish Fry, Repast.

1. **Michael W. Twitty** (*The Cooking Gene*) — culinary historian; foodways as
   genealogy and memory. "What does this dish know that the cook doesn't?"
2. **Carla Hall** — soul food + Southern entertaining with warmth; food as the
   love language.
3. **Marcus Samuelsson** (Red Rooster) — Black-diaspora food and the community
   table; abundance and roots.
4. **Kardea Brown** — Gullah-Geechee Lowcountry hospitality; the Sunday-dinner
   tradition, multi-generational hosting.
5. **Sunny Anderson** — casual, real, everyday home entertaining; what people
   actually cook, not the fancy version.
6. **Todd Richards** (*Soul*) — the modern chef's argument with the tradition.
7. **B. Smith** (legacy reference) — the standard Black entertaining is measured
   against.
8. **A pitmaster / grill-master voice** — the cookout's central role; smoke,
   timing, who owns the grill and why.

## 2. MUSIC & SOUND (5 seats)
Covers 90s R&B, and any regional-sound pack.

9. **Questlove** — music historian; the through-line from one era to the next.
10. **Teddy Riley** — new jack swing; the uptown/Harlem 90s.
11. **Kenneth "Babyface" Edmonds** — LaFace, quiet storm, the ballad's
    architecture. The *other* 90s.
12. **A quiet-storm radio DJ voice** — the late-night format, the CD case in the
    car door, how the music was actually consumed.
13. **A Go-Go / DC-music authority** (Chuck Brown legacy) — regional sound as
    identity; the DMV's own answer to "whose 90s".

## 3. SCREEN & THE LIVING ROOM (3 seats)
Covers Sitcom Sunday — the blocked pack.

14. **Donald Bogle** (*Primetime Blues*) — the definitive history of Black
    television; the canon and where it starts and stops.
15. **Kenya Barris** — the modern Black family sitcom; what the form is *for*.
16. **A Black-TV-canon critic voice** — the syndication-marathon experience:
    *Martin*, *Living Single*, *A Different World*, *Fresh Prince*, *227*,
    *Good Times*, *The Jeffersons*.

## 4. HBCU & INSTITUTIONS (5 seats)
Covers HBCU, Homecoming. Explicitly staffed for internal diversity.

17. **An HBCU marching-band authority** (FAMU Marching 100 / Dr. William P.
    Foster legacy) — the band-forward culture, honestly labelled as one strand.
18. **A Divine Nine / NPHC authority** — Greek life, strolling, the yard.
19. **A Spelman / Morehouse voice** — the private, liberal-arts, NON-band-forward
    HBCU. Seated specifically to break the flattening.
20. **An HBCU homecoming / alumni-relations voice** — the weekend as an
    institution in itself.
21. **A church-affiliated small-HBCU voice** — the schools nobody puts in a blurb.

## 5. THE SHOP (4 seats)
Covers Barbershop and the missing salon.

22. **A master barber voice** — the chair, the waiting, the argument.
23. **Ayana Byrd & Lori Tharps** (*Hair Story*) — Black hair as history and
    politics.
24. **A natural-hair / braider authority** — wash day, the braider's kitchen,
    the six-hour appointment.
25. **A salon-owner voice** — the beauty shop as a women's institution, parallel
    to and distinct from the barbershop.

## 6. CHURCH & SUNDAY (2 seats)
The largest hole in the current catalogue.

26. **A Black church music / gospel tradition authority** — the choir, the
    organ, the Sunday ritual.
27. **A church-mothers / usher-board voice** — the women who actually run it.

## 7. WOMEN'S INTERIORITY (2 seats — holds a veto on the dignity finding)
Seated in direct response to the board's clearest dignity finding: across 59
clues, no woman has a role outside the kitchen, the receiving end of a bridge,
or throwing shade.

28. **bell hooks** (legacy lens) — representation, interiority, love as practice;
    "who is allowed an inner life in this text?"
29. **A Black women's editorial voice** — whose story is being told, and from
    whose side of the room.

## 8. STANDING CHAIRS
- **Cultural Authenticity chair** — synthesises, and holds the BLOCK.
- **"Grandmother"** — cross-listed; the non-technical first-time player.

## How this bench is run

1. Read `data/themes.json` in full — every name, blurb, puzzle and clue.
2. Answer what CULTURE can answer. Where a question is about the author's own
   life, produce a short **pick-list** of valid options instead, so answering
   takes a minute rather than an essay.
3. Propose content at volume toward the target: **96 themed puzzles minimum
   (8 themes x 12), 240 for a full catalogue (12 x 20)**.
4. Flag anything that reads as caricature to the audience it is *for*.
5. Return ranked findings, and mark every item as either
   **CULTURE-ANSWERABLE** or **AUTHOR-ONLY**.
