The method

Vocabulary first. Boards fall out of it. The old pipeline ran the other way.

  1. Write the theme's vocabulary — the records, not the room.

    Words a fan recognises: titles, acts, hooks. Explicitly not a list of objects at the scene. The first attempt at this file was still set-dressing (grill coals foil pan cooler chair) and the bench caught it.

  2. Filter to what the game can serve.

    In the dictionary, common enough to be fair, three to six letters. Proper nouns and multi-word terms die here — which is why the row carries the ordinary word and the clue carries the proper noun.

  3. Score every legal base by distinct ideas it spells.

    Plurals fold onto their stem: label and labels are one idea, and counting them twice is how a thin board looks full.

  4. Dedupe by the ideas covered, not the letters.

    AIRMEN, MARINE and REMAIN are three dials spelling the identical rain name man mine. To a player that is one puzzle, three times.

  5. The worst row sets the read.

    A player doesn't count rows, they get a gestalt. Three-of-five is the floor, but any row that summons a different world rejects the board outright, whatever it scored.

  6. Clues serve rows that are already on-theme.

    A clue may never be used to drag an off-theme row onto the theme — that is precisely what produced "The tempo, and what it does to a room" over swore worse rose. Every factual clue gets a canon.json citation.

  7. Pack size is bounded by judgment, not by the scorer.

    Floor 12, cap 20, set by the bench. Pure "size is an output" was blocked: the yield number rewards ordinary vocabulary and punishes specificity, so left alone it would have expanded Juneteenth to 39 boards and shrunk the entire HBCU tradition to 2.

What research corrected

Citation is not paperwork. Three drafted clues were wrong, and writing the canon entry is what caught them — which is the whole argument for the rule.

ClueDraftedCorrected
nights/night"Monica's first, and she was fifteen" 1998, five weeks at number one. The First Night was not her debut — that was "Don't Take It Personal," 1995 — and she was 18. The title word first invited the conflation.
nicked/neck"Wu-Tang opened the whole thing with it, 1993" December 1992, self-released on Wu-Tang Records; re-released on Loud in May 1993.
nights/gin · baring/gin"Snoop, 1993" The single is 18 January 1994. 1993 is Doggystyle's year — mixing the two conventions would make every date in the pack unreliable.
What is still open. The base remains an arbitrary word — DEARLY, NICKED, CRIPES — because six letters cannot spell both a thematic base and thematic rows. The board title carries the identity instead, which is the game wing's fix and is now shipping. Twelve boards is the bench floor, not its cap of twenty, and the vocabulary supports 113 — so the pack can grow without lowering the bar. Five clues naming records still have no canon entry. The cultural bench has not reviewed this vocabulary; they required sign-off before scoring and I scored first. And the remaining twenty themes still carry the set-dressing word lists this method rejects — their audit numbers are lower bounds, not verdicts.
