## What we are asking you for

This is a word game. Each board is six letters, and every row is a word spelled from them. The clue is what the player reads.

The clues in this pack were written from research and from a structured review process — **not** from community consultation. That distinction is written into the project rules, and you are the part that was missing. You are being paid for judgment, not for proofreading.

The question for every clue is the same: **does this land?**

- If it lands, say so. "It's fine" is a real answer and a useful one.
- If it does not, say what is wrong — inaccurate, flattening, dated, 
  a stereotype, too inside, not inside enough, or just not how anyone talks.
- If it is close, say what would fix it.

You do not need to be fair to the writing. A clue that is merely *fine* is worth flagging if it should have been better.

**⚑ marks a clue with a source behind it.** Those are factual claims — 
if one is wrong, that matters more than a clue that is only flat, and it 
takes a correction to the record as well as to the sentence.
