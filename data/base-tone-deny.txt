# Bases that are legal but wrong as a PRIZE WORD.
#
# The base is the word the player lands on at the moment of winning — it gets
# the ring, the celebration and the share card. So it is not merely a letter
# supply; it is the note the board ends on.
#
# The vocabulary-first method scores a base purely on the rows its letters can
# spell, which is why it happily offered `DEATHS` and `STUPID` as boards for a
# family cookout. Both are legal English, both spell good on-theme rows, and
# both are the wrong thing to celebrate at a table full of relatives.
#
# This is NOT a profanity list — `lib/blocklist.mjs` already handles slurs and
# obscenity, and nothing here is unsayable. These are words that would land
# badly as a triumph, on themes that are warm by construction: a cookout, a
# repast, a homecoming, a church service.
#
# Kept deliberately small and reviewable. A base only belongs here if landing
# on it would make a player wince, not merely shrug.
